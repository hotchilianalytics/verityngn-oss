# Who might care — multimodal claim-risk platform (Nov 2026)

**Purpose:** Interest map for the OSS **3.0.1** public push (PyPI live). **Not** a CRM send sheet.  
**Constraint:** Creator / journalist **cold email remains frozen** (KEY_DECISIONS 2026-07-02). Talk only if someone replies to a public post or already has a warm thread.

**Product shape:** Apache engine turns YouTube or **local** video into claim-level **risk** reports (CRAAP / SIFT packs, counter-intel, optional Deep Research). Hosted gallery / credits on verityindex.com. Not a lie detector, not CCTV surveillance, not earnings alpha.

---

## What we already learned

| Finding | Implication |
|---------|-------------|
| Creators and fact-checkers answered ~0 bookings to cold email | They are **audience** for OSS/HN/gallery — not the buyer |
| Legal deposition + agency sponsor-readiness were the commercial pivot | Buyers need **local file** dossiers and sponsor-readiness packs |
| Academic / policy intros (UW CIP, Stanford SIO) were listed as a YouTube partnership path | Method critique and referrals, not a sales funnel |

Public posts replace introduction email. This memo ranks **who has a structural reason to care** if they discover the release.
